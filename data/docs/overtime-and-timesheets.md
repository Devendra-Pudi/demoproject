# Overtime and timesheet policy
Non-exempt employees must record all hours worked, including overtime, in the time system by the end of each week. Overtime must be approved in advance by a manager.

Overtime is compensated at 1.5 times the standard rate, or with time off in lieu where local law permits.

Timesheet errors must be corrected within the current pay period. Deliberate misreporting of hours is a disciplinary matter.
